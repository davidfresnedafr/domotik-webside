05 — Selected Projects

Quietly powerful. Always intentional.

A glimpse into recent residences and commercial spaces where every layer of technology disappears into the architecture.

01

Luxury Residence Automation

Private Residence · 920 m²

Challenge

A multi-level coastal home with five climate zones, three audio rooms and an architect-led lighting plan — all to be controlled effortlessly by the owners and household staff.

Solution

Unified KNX automation, distributed audio across nine zones, integrated shading, and a single elegant control interface on wall keypads, mobile and tablet.

Result

One-touch scenes for every moment of the day. Energy use down 28%. A property that feels intuitive from the very first arrival.

02

Smart Apartment — Security & Audio

Penthouse · Mixed-Use Tower

Challenge

A high-floor penthouse owner needed discreet 360° camera coverage, biometric entry and concert-grade audio, all installed without disturbing a freshly completed interior.

Solution

Architectural in-ceiling speakers, micro-aperture cameras on the perimeter, fingerprint and mobile entry, plus an enterprise mesh network. All retrofitted in twelve days.

Result

Invisible technology, premium acoustics, and remote oversight — all without a single visible cable or compromise to the design.

03

Commercial Access Control Integration

Corporate Office · 4 Floors

Challenge

A growing firm required floor-by-floor access permissions, visitor management, integrated video surveillance and a network capable of supporting hybrid teams.

Solution

Mobile-credential access on every door, IP camera integration, a segmented enterprise network, and a centralized dashboard for facility managers.

Result

Streamlined operations, audit-ready compliance, and one calm interface where security, network and facilities meet.
